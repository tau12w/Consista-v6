What is a habit tracker?

A habit tracker is a tool that helps you record your progress as you work on building new habits. Instead of relying only on memory, you can see your daily progress and consistency over time.

Track your habits with Consista

Consista keeps habit tracking straightforward. You can create habits that matter to you and track whether you complete them each day.

Why use a habit tracker?

  • Keep track of daily habits
  • Build consistent routines
  • See your progress over time
  • Stay accountable to your goals
  • Keep your habit tracking simple
